English Tenses (၁၂ မျိုး)

1. Present Simple

Subject + Verb(v1/s/es) + Object

Example: She eats an apple every day.

2. Present Continuous

Subject + am/is/are + Verb(-ing)

Example: They are playing football now.

3. Present Perfect

Subject + have/has + Verb(v3)

Example: I have finished my homework.

4. Present Perfect Continuous

Subject + have/has + been + Verb(-ing)

Example: He has been studying for two hours.

5. Past Simple

Subject + Verb(v2) + Object

Example: We visited Yangon last year.

6. Past Continuous

Subject + was/were + Verb(-ing)

Example: I was sleeping when you called.

7. Past Perfect

Subject + had + Verb(v3)

Example: The train had left before I arrived.

8. Past Perfect Continuous

Subject + had + been + Verb(-ing)

Example: They had been waiting for hours.

9. Future Simple

Subject + will/shall + Verb(v1)

Example: I will help you tomorrow.

10. Future Continuous

Subject + will be + Verb(-ing)

Example: At 8 PM, I will be watching TV.

11. Future Perfect

Subject + will have + Verb(v3)

Example: By next week, I will have finished the project.

12. Future Perfect Continuous

Subject + will have been + Verb(-ing)

Example: By 2027, she will have been teaching here for 5 years.
